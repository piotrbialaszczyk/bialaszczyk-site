About Me

I'm a QA Engineer at Samsung Electronics, where I work on validating Android devices across Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and cellular technologies. I spend most of my days in Linux, using the command line, Git and Python to automate repetitive tasks and improve testing workflows. More generally, I'm the kind of person who enjoys figuring out how things work and fixing them when they don't.

Most of the tools I use at work are internal, so this website is where I explore technologies beyond my day job. It's a place for me to experiment with web development, Linux, automation, cloud technologies and AI, while documenting what I learn along the way. I'm learning in public, so don't expect polished tutorials or expert advice, expect experiments, notes and the occasional mistake.

My interest in automation led me to Java first, and later to Python, which quickly became my language of choice for building tools that make work (and sometimes life) a little easier. More recently, I've been exploring AI through the AI devs course, looking at how large language models and autonomous agents can be applied to software testing and development.

When I'm not staring at screens, I'm usually out running. I recently completed my first marathon in 3:52 - a challenge that taught me that consistency beats motivation almost every time. I'm already training for the next one. If you'd like to follow the journey, you can find me on Strava.

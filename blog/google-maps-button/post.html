Google Maps Button went missing - and I accepted it for too long.

A while ago, Google quietly removed the Maps button from search results, likely due to the Digital Markets Act. It annoyed me more than I expected, but I kept ignoring it, until I decided to fix it.

So I wrote a simple Firefox add-on that brings the Maps button back. It works like the old one:

  • Appears next to "All" / "Images" / "News"
  • Auto-localizes (e.g. Mapy in Polish, Plans in French)
  • Adapts to dark mode (sort of!)
  • Works across Google domains (e.g. google.pl, google.de, etc.)

The Tricky Parts

  • Styling the button to match Google's ever-changing CSS was painful. It's still not perfectly aligned or color-matched 100% of the time.
  • I couldn't get it pixel-perfect across Firefox, Firefox Dev, Linux, and Windows. Fixing one setup broke another.
  • Google rewrites the page dynamically, which makes injection a challenge.
